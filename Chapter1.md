## Heading out of the village

Ferris will share that they are then ready to go, but Yae stops him and says that they need to wait.
So you do.
You go to Yaereene's house, a small, nondescript house right underneath the Grandfather Tree. It's somewhat lavishly decorated, with the red plant that lavishly decorated the half-elf and mysterious figure's antlers.

 1-5: Still don't know
 6-10: It's a plant that looks like it belongs in Yae's studio apartment.
